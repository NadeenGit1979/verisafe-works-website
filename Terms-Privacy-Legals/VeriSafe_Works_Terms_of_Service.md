### 6.1 Worker Obligations

Before commencing any recording, the Worker must:

1. Inform the Customer that VeriSafe Works will be used during the visit
2. Explain that the purpose is mutual protection and transparency
3. Obtain the Customer's verbal consent to be recorded (this verbal consent will itself be recorded as the opening of the Session)
4. Confirm Customer consent via the in-App confirmation screen
5. Ensure all persons present in the property are made aware a recording is taking place

Failure to obtain proper consent before recording is a serious breach of these Terms.
