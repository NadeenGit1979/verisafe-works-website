VeriSafe Works — In-Session Consent Notice

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 10 July 2026
For display in-app, at the start of every recorded session, and as a standalone printable document.

What Is This Notice?

This notice explains what VeriSafe Works records during your tradesperson's visit, why, and what happens to your data. Your tradesperson is required to inform you before any recording begins.

What Will Be Recorded?

During this visit, the VeriSafe Works app will record:

  • Audio — conversations between you and the tradesperson about the job
  • Photographs — images of the work area before work begins and after completion
  • Video — where relevant, footage of job conditions or completion
  • A transcript — a text version of the audio recording, created automatically

Why Is It Being Recorded?

VeriSafe Works creates a transparent, verified record of the work carried out in your home. This protects both you and the tradesperson:

For you (the homeowner/resident):
- A clear record of exactly what was agreed
- Protection if there is ever a dispute about what was said or agreed
- A complete record of any changes to the original job scope and your approval of them
- Confirmation of job completion

For the tradesperson:
- Protection against unfair complaints or false claims
- Proof that additional work was properly authorised by you
- Verification of professional conduct throughout the visit

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Refuse — you may decline recording at any time before it begins. The tradesperson must respect this.
  • Stop the recording — you may ask the tradesperson to stop recording at any point during the visit.
  • Receive a copy — you will automatically receive a copy of the audio recording and transcript by email when the job is completed.
  • Request deletion — you may request that your recording be deleted by contacting privacy@verisafe.works (subject to any active dispute).
  • Access your data — you may request all data held about you by contacting privacy@verisafe.works.

Who Holds Your Data?

Your data is held securely by Verisafe Software Limited (trading as "VeriSafe Works"), registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO registration number ZC028351). Your recordings are:

  • Encrypted at rest and in transit, and stored on secure UK/EU cloud servers, with access restricted to authorised staff for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy (e.g. delivering your transcript, resolving a dispute)
  • Not shared with any third party except as required by law or as described in our Privacy Policy
  • Automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Never used for marketing or advertising

Questions?
